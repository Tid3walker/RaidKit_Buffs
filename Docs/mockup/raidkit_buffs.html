RaidKit Buffs – Mockup 0.8.2

How to use the mockup

Bar icon: Ctrl+click = use (default, can be switched off in the options), right-click = remove the buff (simulates /cancelaura). Shift+drag = move, Shift+right-click = options. Options: drag items from the catalog into the row, reorder them in the row, drag them out or right-click = remove. Rows: + = empty row or template, ->+ = copy row, eye = show/hide in the bar. Rename with the pencil or a double-click. Simulated class: Warrior.

Profiles: The character always edits its own settings. "Save as ..." creates a named copy, "Load" copies a saved profile (all of it or only rows/appearance) into them. The position of the bar belongs to the character and is never saved or loaded. To try it: "Create example profiles".

Groups: "+ Group" in the row list creates an empty group next to the first group (shown as a placeholder while editing); drag rows onto a group header (and back). Click on the header = group settings (frame, names, buff cap tint, orientation, size). With the window open, drag the icons of a group on the bar (otherwise Shift+drag): close to another group it snaps (green outline) and moves along with it from then on. "Unwanted Buffs", "Other Buffs" and "Debuffs" are groups of their own (end of the row list): with the window open they show as a preview box even when empty and can be dragged; otherwise they disappear without content and whatever hangs on them moves up.

Buffs: Every row takes consumables and buffs from others (the catalog tabs only switch the list). Buff icons (e.g. in World Buffs, Raid Buffs) are gray or colored; you cannot use them (exception: your own class buffs such as Battle Shout). Buff cap: from 4 free slots the background of the bar turns yellow, then orange, at 1 red. Below the bar: Unwanted Buffs (click on the X removes all, in combat too), Other Buffs with all other active buffs and the Debuffs. To try it: "Raid situation", then "Procs" a few times. At the cap the oldest buff drops off, except flask, Zanza and world buffs; a lost consumable flashes red and reports in the chat.
